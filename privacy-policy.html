Privacy Policy — Chop Chop Flashlight

Effective: September 29, 2026

Chop Chop Flashlight ("the app") lets you toggle your phone's flashlight with a double chop-chop motion. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: it collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

Information we collect

None. The app does not create accounts, does not use analytics, does not serve ads, and does not include any third-party SDKs. The app does not even request internet access, so it has no technical means of transmitting data off your device.

Motion sensor data

To detect the chop-chop gesture, the app reads your phone's accelerometer in real time. This sensor data is processed entirely on your device, in memory, the moment it is read. It is never recorded, never stored, and never transmitted.

On-device settings

Your preferences (whether gesture detection is enabled, your calibration thresholds, and whether haptic feedback is on) are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. They are never uploaded or shared.

Information sharing

We share no information with anyone, because we have no information to share.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
